The game originally launched in 2010 and received such harsh reviews that Square Enix publicly apologized and suspended the monthly subscription fee.
In version 1.0, a simple flowerpot had as many polygons and textures as a playable character, which made the game crash on most computers.
Naoki Yoshida was rushed in to take over as director and decided to destroy the old world within the game's own story in order to relaunch it from scratch.
To shut down the version 1.0 servers in 2012, the developers made the red moon Dalamud literally fall onto the world of Eorzea.
Trapped inside Dalamud was the ancient dragon Bahamut, who escaped and devastated the continent in the famous End of an Era cinematic.
Anyone who paid for at least 90 days of subscription in version 1.0 received an exclusive tattoo on their character's back and pays a cheaper monthly fee forever.
Yoshi-P has publicly rejected the idea of creating a nostalgia server for version 1.0, describing the original experience as a "nightmare".
In the old version, opening your inventory required slow, direct communication with the central server, causing annoying delays.
The original game punished long play sessions by reducing the experience gained to zero after a certain amount of time.
The August 2013 relaunch was such an immediate success that Square Enix had to temporarily halt digital sales to relieve the servers.
The ending credits of A Realm Reborn last more than 1 hour and 30 minutes because they include the names of thousands of version 1.0 players.
FFXIV entered the Guinness World Records as the video game with the largest number of original music tracks, surpassing hundreds of compositions.
Masayoshi Soken composed much of the Shadowbringers and Endwalker soundtrack straight from a hospital bed while being treated for cancer.
The congestion at Endwalker's launch was so heavy that the game had queues lasting hours and sales were suspended again.
The game's Free Trial lets you play the base campaign and the first two expansions in full with no time limit.
Completing only the Main Scenario Quest from level 1 to 100 takes hundreds of hours of continuous play.
The game has hundreds of unique bosses spread across dungeons, trials and raids.
FFXIV has won "Best Community Support" at The Game Awards multiple times.
The game has received major expansions regularly for over a decade, including Heavensward, Stormblood, Shadowbringers, Endwalker and Dawntrail.
The title has full cross-play between PC, macOS, PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S and Switch 2.
The game's casino is a direct recreation of the iconic amusement park from Final Fantasy VII.
The collectible card game inside the Gold Saucer comes straight from Final Fantasy VIII, keeping classic rules such as Random and Plus.
The entire level 50 raid series is a direct tribute to the climax of Final Fantasy III, including bosses such as Cloud of Darkness.
The Stormblood expansion raids are set in the universe of Final Fantasy Tactics and Final Fantasy XII, with a script written by Yasumi Matsuno himself.
In the Omega raid series, players face the Phantom Train from Final Fantasy VI, although it is not possible to suplex it.
The main villains of FFVI and FFV appear as raid bosses with their laughs and original musical themes remixed.
The Shadowbringers raid series was created in partnership with Yoko Taro and brings the characters 2B, 9S and A2 to the universe of Eorzea.
The first boss of the Omega: Alphascape series is Chaos from Final Fantasy I.
The mechanical mounts of A Realm Reborn play Terra's theme (FFVI) while you ride across the map.
The franchise's classic duo appears as mechanical engineers loyal to Cid Garlond from the very beginning of the adventure.
You don't need to create alternate characters, since a single avatar can master every combat, gathering and crafting class.
Changing class is as simple as equipping the weapon that corresponds to that profession from your inventory.
Culinarians, blacksmiths and alchemists have their own skill rotations and complete story campaigns.
The Blue Mage is a "limited job" that learns spells by watching monsters use attacks and cannot join regular dungeon queues.
Logging out in cities or near Aetheryte crystals accumulates an experience bonus shown as a half-moon on the XP bar.
Two players can hold an official wedding ceremony (Eternal Bonding) complete with cutscenes, guests and a two-seat white chocobo.
The game offers an item called Fantasia that lets you completely change your character's race, gender and appearance at any time.
At the launch of A Realm Reborn, male Miqo'te and female Roegadyn did not exist in version 1.0, but were added at the fans' request.
These two races were added in Shadowbringers initially locked to a single gender each, but gained their opposite genders in later updates.
Because of the shape of their ears and heads, these two races cannot display most of the game's helmets and hats.
Bard players unlock musical instruments and can play notes in real time to put on shows in city plazas.
The visual gear transmutation system is so deep that the community jokes that putting together outfits is the true final challenge.
The built-in photo mode lets you pause animations, add colored lights, apply filters and even make the character look at the camera.
You can breed chocobos at the Gold Saucer to produce chicks with better genetic traits for racing.
The Lord of Verminion minigame turns collectible minions into units of a real-time strategy game.
The name of the main continent derives from "Eos", the Greek goddess of the dawn.
The heroes' organization was formed by merging Louisoix's Circle of Knowing with the Path of the Twelve organization.
The language spoken by the dragons in Heavensward was created from scratch by the writers, with its own grammar and vocabulary.
Detective Hildibrand's side quests are famous for their exaggerated facial expressions that test the limits of the graphics engine.
Several NPCs naturally say or mention the name you chose in their written dialogue.
The city-state of Ishgard was strongly inspired by European Gothic architecture and the Hundred Years' War.
The names of the Ascian villains (such as Lahabrea, Emet-Selch and Elidibus) are the same as the Esper bosses of Final Fantasy XII.
Despite their childlike appearance, Lalafell are fully grown adults, and many of them are the shrewdest merchants and politicians in Ul'dah.
The ancient Allagan Empire is responsible for almost all the advanced technology, clones and monster prisons found in Eorzea.
Unlike many MMOs where heroes always survive, FFXIV became famous for killing off beloved characters from the main group.
The Shadowbringers expansion takes place on "The First", a parallel world that was almost completely destroyed by an excess of Light instead of Darkness.
In Shadowbringers, the protagonist takes the title of Warrior of Darkness to restore night to a world blinded by eternal light.
The 2021 expansion's title refers to walking to the end of the journey begun in 2013, closing the arc of Hydaelyn and Zodiark.
In the Endwalker expansion, the player travels thousands of years into the past to see the ancient world before the great sundering.
The continent of Dawntrail was inspired by the cultures and landscapes of Central and South America.
An NPC named Edda appears as a ghost in random and extremely rare appearances in the main cities during the game's early-morning hours.
In the Eureka zones there are notorious monsters like Cassie that drop items at such low drop rates that they are worth millions of Gil.
The Cassie Earring and the Copycat Bulb minion are among the rarest and most coveted items in the game's history.
Yoshi-P has admitted he regrets adding Cloud Strife's motorcycle to the game because it breaks the medieval immersion the first time a player enters Eorzea.
The director also said he found it tricky to have created Hilda, the only half-Hyur, half-Elezen character, because it led to constant requests for more hybrid races.
The Fat Chocobo mount doesn't fly by flapping its wings but by chasing a piece of cake hanging from a pole in front of its face.
Players can craft a double bed that floats through the skies while the character sleeps comfortably.
The Wind-up Haurchefant minion has a touching description that honors one of the most loyal allies in the story.
Many item and achievement descriptions contain puns on the names of famous rock songs, movies and books.
There is a black cat on the rooftops of Ul'dah that turns into a giant bat if you watch it for long enough.
The community popularized the meme that the free trial goes up to level 70 with no time limit, used to convince friends to play.
Players buy mansions in the residential districts and turn them into bars, cafés, art galleries and nightclubs with live DJs on Twitch.
When entering any dungeon with a party, players usually type o/ in chat, an emoticon representing a person waving.
At the end of a dungeon, almost everyone types tyfp (thank you for party) before leaving.
It is an unwritten community rule to wait patiently while new players watch the final cutscenes of a dungeon before attacking the boss.
New players get a plant sprout icon (Sprout) next to their name, signaling to veterans that they should be patient and help.
Experienced players who meet strict requirements earn a crown icon, although the community jokes that many just want it out of vanity.
On several servers, Lalafell players organize synchronized marches through the capitals wearing identical outfits.
When the creator of Berserk passed away in 2021, thousands of players dressed as Dark Knights formed giant lines in the cities to honor him.
During the 2021 digital Fan Fest, director Naoki Yoshida cried live while revealing that composer Masayoshi Soken had secretly beaten cancer.
In the transition from A Realm Reborn to Heavensward, practically the entire English voice cast was replaced by British actors.
The English localization uses distinct regional British accents to differentiate characters from Limsa Lominsa, Gridania and Ul'dah.
Masayoshi Soken formed an official rock band called The Primals made up of developers from the game's own team.
The theme song Answers, sung by Susan Calloway, was composed by the legendary Nobuo Uematsu.
Several boss songs are not sung by hired artists but by members of Square Enix's audio and localization team.
The Leviathan battle music had to be hurriedly re-recorded because the first version seemed too calm for a sea fight.
The director appears in the game as an NPC called the Wandering Minstrel, who tells exaggerated versions of boss fights (creating the Extreme and Ultimate fights).
Naoki Yoshida's personal in-game avatar is a Lalafell Black Mage named Yoshi'p Sampo.
Before Shadowbringers, a leaker known as BluFever correctly revealed almost everything new about the expansion months before the official announcement.
Although they are an icon of the series, chocobos in FFXIV have a characteristic smell constantly mentioned by NPCs in dialogue.
Ultimate Raids are the hardest challenges in the game, lasting nearly 20 minutes without checkpoints and requiring perfect execution from 8 players.
Professional guilds compete live for days on end without sleeping to be the first in the world to defeat a new Ultimate.
Completing an Ultimate Raid rewards the player with a token that can be exchanged for one of the game's brightest, flashiest weapons.
Dungeons like Palace of the Dead have 200 randomly generated floors where players start from level 1 again.
Completing all 200 floors of Palace of the Dead completely solo and without dying grants one of the hardest titles in the entire MMO.
The game forbids the use of external programs to measure the damage dealt by players (DPS meters) to avoid toxicity in chat.
Critical hits and Direct Hits can happen on the same strike, producing a "Critical Direct Hit" that shows double exclamation marks on screen.
Giant monsters called S-Rank Hunts only appear on the map when players meet specific secret conditions, such as throwing trash on the ground or dying in a certain place.
Buying your own house is so competitive that the game uses a lottery system in cycles of several days to distribute empty plots.
Even after ending its ten-year main saga in Endwalker, the game began a new ten-year narrative era starting with Dawntrail.
